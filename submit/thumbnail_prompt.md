# 🎨 YouTube Viral Thumbnail Prompt (16:9)

> **Tool:** Google Nano Banana / Midjourney / DALL-E / Flux  
> **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (`--ar 16:9`)  
> **Instructions:** You can optionally attach a picture of yourself or a team member to make the thumbnail personalized with a real expressive human face.

---

## Prompt for Nano Banana / Image Generator

```text
A cinematic, high-contrast YouTube thumbnail in 16:9 aspect ratio designed to maximize click-through rate.

Left side: A split visual showing a distressed software engineer or developer reacting in shock at 2 AM, illuminated by the harsh red emergency glow of glowing terminal monitors displaying cascading error codes and "HTTP 503 SERVICE UNAVAILABLE" in bold glitching text. Red warning alerts and siren icons pulse in the dark room.

Right side: A sleek, futuristic cyberpunk neon-blue and purple glowing holographic brain / neural memory node representing Hindsight AI memory, with a glowing green shield stopping an exploding database crash. Large bold high-impact text in YouTube thumbnail font: "DON'T SCALE!" with a red prohibited warning sign, contrasted with a glowing green badge reading "RECALLED FIX".

Style: High-end tech YouTube aesthetics (reminiscent of Fireship, NetworkChuck, or MKBHD thumbnails). Dramatic rim lighting, ultra-sharp detail, deep blacks, high saturation neon red vs neon green/indigo contrasts, professional depth of field, 8k resolution, cinematic composition. No cluttered small text.
```

## Quick Nano Banana / Canva Overlay Guide

If you generate the background art with an AI tool and add the text in Canva / Photoshop:

1. **Background:** Dark server room or terminal screen with red alarm glow.
2. **Face / Expression:** Cutout photo of yourself pointing or looking shocked at the screen on one third of the frame.
3. **Primary Text Overlay (Huge, 3 words max):**
   - **`STOP SCALING!`** (Yellow / Red gradient with black drop shadow)
   - or **`AI KILLED MY DB`** (Bold white on red badge)
4. **Icon / Asset:** Add a subtle glowing brain icon with the Hindsight purple hue next to a green checkmark rollback command.
