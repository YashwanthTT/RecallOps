## 🚀 Step-by-Step Publishing Guide

### Step 1: Publish the Article
1. Pick your platform: **Dev.to**, **Medium**, **Hashnode**, or **Substack**.
2. Copy the content from [submit/article.md](file:///Users/yashwanth/Developer/Hackathon/MircosoftHindsight/submit/article.md).
3. Select your favorite title from [submit/titles.md](file:///Users/yashwanth/Developer/Hackathon/MircosoftHindsight/submit/titles.md) (Recommended: `Why our incident agent remembers bad fixes using Hindsight`).
4. Ensure the three required links are present and active:
   - Hindsight GitHub: `https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight`
   - Hindsight Docs: `https://hindsight.vectorize.io/`
   - Vectorize Agent Memory: `https://vectorize.io/what-is-agent-memory`
5. Publish to a public, linkable URL.

### Step 2: Share on Reddit
Submit a **Link post** with your article URL to one of these subreddits:
- [r/llmdevs](https://reddit.com/r/llmdevs)
- [r/sideproject](https://reddit.com/r/sideproject)
- [r/aiagents](https://reddit.com/r/aiagents)
- [r/aimemory](https://reddit.com/r/aimemory)

### Step 3: Publish the LinkedIn Social Post
1. Copy the text from [submit/linkedin_post.md](file:///Users/yashwanth/Developer/Hackathon/MircosoftHindsight/submit/linkedin_post.md).
2. Insert your repo link (`https://github.com/YashwanthTT/MircosoftHindsight`) or published article link.
3. Post it on LinkedIn.
4. **Immediately add a first comment** linking to the Hindsight GitHub repo:
   `Here is the Hindsight repo if you want to check it out: https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight`

### Step 4: Record & Publish the Video to YouTube
1. Use OBS, Loom, or QuickTime.
2. Follow the concise app-only script and cues in [submit/video_script.md](file:///Users/yashwanth/Developer/Hackathon/MircosoftHindsight/submit/video_script.md).
3. Generate a thumbnail using [submit/thumbnail_prompt.md](file:///Users/yashwanth/Developer/Hackathon/MircosoftHindsight/submit/thumbnail_prompt.md) on Google Nano Banana.
4. Upload as a public YouTube video with one of the 5 suggested titles.
